### 1) A plasma photocathode for spin-polarized electron beams using excited hydrogen halides

- DOI：https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2608.04932
- 来源：https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.04932

### 2) Laser-Plasma Accelerator Beams in Light Sources: Femtosecond High-Brightness Radiation through Chirped Pulse Injection

- DOI：https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2608.04699

### 3) Polarization-resolved attosecond gamma-ray emission from few-cycle laser interactions with cone targets

- DOI：https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2608.04363
- 来源：https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.04363
- 价值：QED-PIC 提出锥靶阿秒 MeV γ 源，报告 300 as、最高 6 MeV、最高能端 0.88 线偏振度，并显式量化靶角与激光参数下的通量--能量--偏振折中。
