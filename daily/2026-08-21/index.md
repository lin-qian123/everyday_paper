### 1) Bright dual-pulse betatron X-ray generation from a laser wakefield accelerator

- DOI：[10.48550/arXiv.2608.17555](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2608.17555)
- 来源：[arXiv 摘要页](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.17555)

### 2) Relative and absolute dosimetric commissioning of the ELIMAIA--ELIMED laser-driven proton beamline at 23.45 MeV

- DOI：[10.48550/arXiv.2608.15272](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2608.15272)
- 来源：[arXiv 摘要页](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.15272)

### 3) Comparative study of nonperturbative electron-positron pair production by intense laser fields colliding with either bremsstrahlung gamma-rays or relativistic ions

- DOI：[10.48550/arXiv.2608.18718](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2608.18718)

- 价值：解析建立“电子束—薄转换靶韧致辐射 γ—强激光”的非线性 Breit--Wheeler 方案与相对论离子 Bethe--Heitler 方案的率学对应，为强场 QED 实验设计提供转换靶谱形的理论边界。
